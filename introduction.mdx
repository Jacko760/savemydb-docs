savemydb is a managed backup service for your databases. Install a lightweight agent on the server where your database lives, connect your database, choose where backups are stored, and savemydb handles the rest — encrypted, scheduled, and monitored from one dashboard.

## How it works
<Info>
Built for SQL Server teams. SaveMyDB also supports PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Firebird and other databases.
</Info>
<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and download the agent">
    Create a free account and download the savemydb agent for your operating system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the agent on your server">
    Install it on the machine where your database runs. It connects securely back to savemydb — no inbound ports or firewall changes needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your database">
    Add your database connection details from the dashboard. Credentials are encrypted and never leave your control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your storage">
    Use savemydb's fully managed storage, or connect your own S3 or Dropbox account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a backup job">
    Set a schedule — hourly, daily, or custom — and savemydb takes it from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>
